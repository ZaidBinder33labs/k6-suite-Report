What to look at next

Baseline is the odd one

At a single VU and 0.57 req/s it still took 69 rate-limited responses out of 104, and its p95 of 827 ms sits twelve times above its own median. Either the cap is shared across clients rather than per-key, or the baseline script is issuing requests faster than its stated rate. Worth confirming before the other runs are read as clean.

Dropped iterations

Stress dropped 9 and soak dropped 412 iterations, which means k6 could not start work on schedule with the VUs allocated. Raise preAllocatedVUs and re-run — soak's 9.54/s should land on 10/s once the generator keeps up.

Tail latency under burst

Spike recorded a 10.9 s maximum against a 55 ms median. One slow response during the ramp is normal for connection setup, but if it repeats across runs it is worth isolating which endpoint produced it.
